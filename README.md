# LearnForge notebooks

The Jupyter notebooks and datasets of the LearnForge lessons, for Google Colab. Open a notebook from its lesson's
"Open in Colab" button: it runs in your own Google account, and *File → Save a copy in Drive* keeps your changes.

`student_data_150.csv` holds 8 real students and 142 synthetic ones (marked in its `Source` column).

This repository is generated from the lessons; changes made here are overwritten.
